?How to use this page
  1. Choose your level at the top. You can change it at any time.
  2. Open a situation. Each one has the same 5 steps:
    1. Dialogue – listen and read. Press Play all or one line at a time.
    2. Check understanding – answer the questions, then show the answers.
    3. Practice – complete the sentences. Click a word to hear the sentence.
    4. Role-play – work with a partner. Then switch roles.
    5. Your turn – speak without the script. Something unexpected happens!
  3. At the end, check My progress (“I can…”).

The three levels

  • Level 1 · I can say it Survival (A1–A2): Reproduce → Substitute → Respond. Repeat the model, change some words, answer short questions.
  • Level 2 · I can interact Functional (A2–B1): Adapt → Expand → Negotiate meaning. Change the model, add details and reasons, ask and check.
  • Level 3 · I can improvise Communicative (B1–B2): Interpret → React → Build meaning. Understand the situation, react to surprises, build the conversation together.

Helpful tools

  • Hide text: listen first without reading. Move the mouse over a line to see it.
  • Pause after each line: you have time to repeat each line aloud.
  • A− / A+: change the text size.
Voices and speedChecking the voices on this device…

The voices come from your device or browser, so they work without internet. We use American English voices. If a voice is British (or another accent), the page says so next to the speaker’s name. Men’s lines use a male voice and women’s lines use a female voice.
